# Product Requirements

## Primary users

| User | Primary job |
|---|---|
| Political strategist | Find races, people, districts, and relevant audiences; decide where and when to communicate |
| Media buyer | Build, activate, pace, and optimize CTV campaigns |
| Audience analyst | Import, segment, suppress, approve, and monitor audience data |
| Creative producer | Prepare compliant video creative and assign it to campaign strategies |
| Compliance reviewer | Verify sponsor, disclaimer, authorization, creative, and jurisdiction requirements |
| Campaign manager | Monitor outcomes, approvals, spending, and deliverables |
| Executive / client | Read clear performance and budget reports |
| Developer / partner | Integrate authorized audiences, campaign operations, events, and reporting |

## MVP capabilities

1. Secure organizations, clients, users, roles, and audit logs.
2. Political directory search with source-backed profiles and saved searches.
3. Geography and election/race data with versioned boundaries.
4. Campaign creation, strategy-level targeting, budgets, schedules, creative assignments, and approvals.
5. First-party audience ingest with consent metadata, suppression, access policies, and segment versioning.
6. CTV delivery integration only through licensed supply/DSP relationships.
7. Impression, completion, pacing, spend, and aggregate conversion reporting.
8. Political-ad compliance checklist and review workflow.
9. CSV/API data export with permission checks and export audit events.
10. Dashboard for active campaigns, pending approvals, budget risk, and delivery anomalies.

## Key workflows

### Launch a campaign

1. Create/select organization and political client.
2. Select election, contest, objective, budget, and dates.
3. Configure geography and approved audience segments.
4. Upload/select creatives and submit compliance package.
5. Run preflight checks: audience eligibility, budget, disclosure, inventory, date, and frequency constraints.
6. Approve, activate through licensed delivery integration, and record immutable deployment state.
7. Monitor pacing, performance, exceptions, and change history.

### Research a race

1. Search a candidate, office, district, committee, issue, or ballot measure.
2. Review relationships, source documents, geography, election cycle, committees, spending, and related entities.
3. Save watchlist/query and subscribe to defined changes.
4. Export only data the user is authorized to access.

## Success metrics

- Time to create an approved campaign.
- Percentage of launches passing compliance preflight on first submission.
- Delivery pacing accuracy.
- Report freshness and reconciliation accuracy.
- Search relevance and entity-resolution precision.
- Segment import success rate and policy-validation failure rate.
- Mean time to identify and resolve delivery anomalies.
- Audit completeness for every approval, data export, audience change, and campaign mutation.
