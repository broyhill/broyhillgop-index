# Vibe.co Public Analysis and Clean-Room Boundary

## Scope

This memo records publicly available product signals relevant to designing an independent political CTV platform. It is a comparative product analysis, not a technical extraction. No private systems, code, credentials, protected APIs, customer data, ad inventory contracts, identity graph, models, or copyrighted interface assets should be copied or obtained.

## Publicly observable capabilities

- Vibe describes itself as a self-service Streaming/Connected TV advertising platform.
- Its political vertical presents CTV advertising for campaigns with targeting and performance-oriented workflow.
- Its public materials describe geographic controls including county and congressional-district targeting.
- Public updates describe a direct L2 DataMapping audience-activation partnership for voter and consumer audiences.
- Its public developer materials describe APIs around campaigns, budgets, targeting, audience synchronization, segments, conversions, attribution, event tracking, reporting, and exports.
- Public engineering recruitment material describes performance engineering around bidding, pacing, prediction, auction mechanics, identity matching, distributed systems, and technologies in the general class of Redis/Kafka; job listings also reference modern web/backend engineering.

## Product implications

A viable independent product needs more than a dashboard. It needs source-governed political intelligence, consent- and contract-aware audience workflow, compliant campaign operations, licensed delivery integrations, measurable outcomes, and operational controls. The initial commercial differentiation can be stronger political data governance and directory intelligence rather than attempting to replicate a mature bidding stack.

## Do not copy

- Private or reverse-engineered APIs, app routes, source maps, client bundles, models, or internal documentation.
- Vibe trademarks, design system, UI/UX assets, copy, brand, proprietary campaign logic, or data products.
- Nonpublic inventory, pricing, supply relationships, targeting taxonomies, identity linkages, or customer-specific methods.

## Independent path

Build with independently authored requirements, licensed data providers, contractual media/DSP partners, documented data provenance, original interface design, independent algorithms, and legal/policy review.
