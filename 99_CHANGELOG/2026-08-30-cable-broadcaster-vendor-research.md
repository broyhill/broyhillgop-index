# 2026-08-30 — Cable Broadcaster Online Deployment Vendor Research

## Added

Added `06_TV-BROADCAST/research/vibe-tv-broadcast-vendors.md`.

## Scope

The report maps the vendor layers used to deploy cable broadcaster services online: cloud, contribution/encoding, packaging/origin, playout, CDNs, OTT platforms, entitlement and DRM, ad decisioning, SSAI, audience/measurement, FAST distribution, and consumer endpoints.

## Recommendation

The Ecosystem should build its differentiated political intelligence, directory, data governance, compliance, campaign workflow, and reporting capabilities internally, while using contractual partners for broadcast playout, licensed inventory, video delivery, SSAI, and FAST distribution.
